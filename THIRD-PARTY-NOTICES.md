# Third-Party Components

Aries source is licensed under Apache-2.0. Third-party software keeps its own license and is not relicensed by this repository.

Notable integrations include Hermes CLI, FastAPI/Uvicorn, Volatility 3, Dissect, Eric Zimmerman tools, and 7-Zip. Some are installed from their publishers or discovered on the workstation rather than bundled. Review each component's license and redistribution terms before distributing an installer or appliance containing it.
